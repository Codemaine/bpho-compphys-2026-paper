## About

This is the write-up submitted for the 2026 BPhO Computational Physics Challenge
(Quantum Mechanics theme). It covers ten computational models, from introductory
topics like random walks and Brownian motion to more advanced ones — electron
diffraction, entangled photons, and hydrogenic orbitals.

It also covers four extensions: a derivation of the uncertainty product for a
particle in a box, an evaluation of the CHSH inequality, a look at superpositions
of hydrogenic orbitals, and full solutions to problem sheets QM1–QM3. Results are
validated throughout against standard formulas and expected theoretical limits.

The interactive web app referenced in the paper — which visualises all ten systems
in real time — is at **[bpho.antwi.dev](https://bpho.antwi.dev)**.
